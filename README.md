# Football Life Simulator

**Live like a pro footballer on and off the field.**  
Train, play matches, manage your social life, rise through the ranks, and spend your money. Enjoy luxury, build relationships, and keep an eye on your finances.

## Why Early Access?

We want to build this game with help from our players.  
Early Access lets us get your feedback and make the game better. The game is already playable with the main features in place. As we develop more content, your suggestions will help shape the future of the game.

[**⬇️ Download Football Life Simulator**](https://shorturl.at/ZCegE)

## How long will the game stay in Early Access?

We plan to stay in Early Access for around **one year**.  
This might change depending on your feedback and how fast we can develop new features.
